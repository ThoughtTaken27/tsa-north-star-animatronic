# Historical Context — "The North Star"

The choice of Harriet Tubman and "The North Star" as the focus of this animatronic project is a strategic intersection of historical narrative and physical mechanism. This document details the historical context, the creative choices behind the presentation, and why this theme captured the judges' panel.

---

## 1. Why Harriet Tubman?

Harriet Tubman (born Araminta Ross, c. 1822 – March 10, 1913) was an American abolitionist and social activist. Born into slavery, she escaped and subsequently made some 13 missions to rescue approximately 70 enslaved people, including family and friends, using the network of antislavery activists and safe houses known as the Underground Railroad.

### The Engineering Narrative Hook
In engineering, we solve physical design constraints under strict environmental limitations. Harriet Tubman was, in a real sense, a master systems planner and navigator:
- **Constraint Management:** Operating under absolute secrecy with zero margin of error (any mistake meant capture or death).
- **Environmental Navigation:** Reading physical landscapes, utilizing astronomical indicators (the North Star), and anticipating human patrol schedules.
- **Fail-Safe Protocols:** Establishing strict instructions for escape parties (such as carrying a sedative for infants to protect noise thresholds, and enforcing non-negotiable commitment rules).

---

## 2. "The North Star" Concept

The animatronic is titled "The North Star" after the celestial body (Polaris) that Underground Railroad navigators used to guide their journey northward. 

### Presentation Mechanics
- **Visual Contrast:** The bust features a dark, unpolished outer aesthetic contrasted by a single glowing blue hydraulic fluid path inside the clear tubes visible behind the neck pivot. This symbolizes the hidden, active "life-blood" pathway of the escape network.
- **Auditory Timeline:** The voice file stored in the DFPlayer Mini narrates a brief, high-tension excerpt describing a night escape near the Choptank River in Maryland. 
- **Physical Sync:** The head rises (via the master-slave hydraulic syringes) precisely as the speech reaches its narrative climax, symbolizing rising determination and looking toward the night sky for celestial guidance.

## 3. Historical Accuracy vs. Mechanical Constraint

Recreating a historical figure inside a mechanical framework introduces severe material boundaries:
1.  **Silicone Flesh Tone & Texturing:** Representing age and environmental weathering on soft poured platinum silicone without creating excessive skin thickness that would bind the high-torque neck servos.
2.  **Structural Bone Proportions:** Scaling the internal 3D-printed skull cap to match historical photo plates while maintaining enough volumetric space inside for the dual SG90 eye-shifter rails and the jaw hinge linkage.
3.  **Period Clothing:** Sourcing and tailoring 19th-century cotton-wool textiles that drape naturally over the MDF torso framework without introducing static drag on the lower neck-pan servo.
