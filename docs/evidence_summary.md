## Photo-Verified Build Details

The four build photos in `docs/assets/` independently confirm the following, and are treated as authoritative for as-built state where they conflict with pre-competition planning notes:

- Plywood base painted white, standing on four short wooden dowel feet.
- Arduino Uno (blue ELEGOO board), 400-tie-point breadboard, DFPlayer Mini seated on the breadboard, electrolytic capacitor on the servo rail, 3 W speaker, 9V battery with barrel adapter, and the switched 4xAA holder all mounted openly on the base.
- HC-SR501 PIR at the front of the base with both potentiometers and the trigger-mode jumper visible.
- MG996R drive servo pushing the base syringe plunger through a wooden dowel taped to the servo horn; the syringe is zip-tied to the base.
- A second 60 mL syringe mounted at the shoulder with vinyl tubing running to it — confirming the two-syringe master/slave circuit.
- No servo body at the right shoulder joint. The arm pivots on a plain metal pin through a drilled hole; the joint is entirely passive. Pre-competition planning notes had floated using a gutted SG90 body as the dead hinge, which is not what was built.
- SG90 lantern arm servo zip-tied to the opposite end of the shoulder crossbar, wires intact and connected.
- Craft-stick lantern frame, painted foam ball head with fabric headwrap, brown fabric shell, and a "HARRIET TUBMAN" placard on the base front.
- Black foam-board backdrop with painted/speckled stars.
- 4xAA holder loaded with NiMH rechargeable cells, so the servo rail was nearer 4.8V nominal than the 6V an alkaline set would give.
