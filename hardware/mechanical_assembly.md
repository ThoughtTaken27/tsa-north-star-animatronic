# Mechanical Assembly & Structural Guide

This document details the mechanical construction, frame rigging, dual-axis neck joint, and skeletal assembly for "The North Star" animatronic Harriet Tubman bust.

---

## 1. Internal Frame & Torso Assembly

The base of the animatronic is a rigid box-chassis designed to support the static weight of the outer bust and handle the dynamic load generated by high-torque joint accelerations.

### Materials & Fabrication
- **Structural Medium:** 6mm thick Medium-Density Fiberboard (MDF) selected for its dimensional stability and ease of rapid mechanical tooling.
- **Jointing Method:** Interlocking t-slot joints reinforced with wood glue and M4 steel machine bolts.
- **Torso Dimensions:** 350mm (width) $\times$ 220mm (depth) $\times$ 450mm (height), matching average human female shoulder proportions.

## 2. Dual-Axis Neck Universal Joint (U-Joint)

To allow both vertical tilting (Pitch) and side-to-side rotation (Yaw) without mechanical binding, the neck uses a custom-rigged aluminum dual-axis universal joint.

### Mechanical Rigging
- **Center of Rotation:** The U-joint is centered at the throat line to mimic natural human atlas-axial joint movement.
- **Neck Yaw (Head Pan):** Driven by an MG996R servo mounted horizontally inside the lower torso. A direct linkage rod drives the lower pivot of the U-joint.
- **Neck Pitch (Head Tilt):** Driven by vertical slave hydraulic syringes linked directly to the rear neck plate, bypassing standard gears to achieve smooth movement.

---

## 3. Skull & Face Skeletal Mounts

The head is built around a lightweight 3D-printed skull cap that anchors internal actuators and supports the outer silicone skin shell.

### Internal Actuator Rigging
1.  **Jaw Axis (Mouth Flaps):** A pivot axle runs transversely across the jaw line. An MG996R servo drives a direct linkage arm connected to the lower chin plate, controlling mouth openings in sync with the audio sequencer.
2.  **Eye-Shifter Rail (Eye Move):** Dual acrylic eyes are mounted on a common linkage bar. An SG90 micro servo drives the bar side-to-side to execute rapid, lifelike eye shifting.
3.  **PTFE Friction Treatment:** Dry-film PTFE (Teflon) spray lubricant was applied to the skull pivots and jaw axles. This reduces mechanical drag, keeping current draw on the 6V motor rail low.
