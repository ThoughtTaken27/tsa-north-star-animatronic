+----------------------------------------------------------------------+
| 1. POWER SYSTEM: SEPARATE SOURCES WITH COMMON GROUND                 |
+----------------------------------------------------------------------+

  ARDUINO POWER (5V via USB)
  +─────────────────+       [VCC 5V] (from USB) ==> Arduino 5V rail
  │ LAPTOP / USB    │       [GND 0V]  (from USB) ==> Arduino GND
  │ POWER BANK      │
  +─────────────────+

  SERVO POWER (6V via Battery Pack)
  +─────────────────+
  │  4×AA BATTERY   │  (1.5V × 4 = 6V)
  │  PACK (6V)      ├───────┐
  +─────────────────+       │
                            │
                            ▼
                      +-----------+
                      │ 1000uF    │ 25V Electrolytic Capacitor
                      │ CAPACITOR │ (across + and - rails)
                      +-----------+
                        │      │
                     (6V+)  (GND)
                        │      │
                        ▼      ▼
                     =============  Servo Power Rail
                      6V+    GND

  COMMON GROUND POINT
  Arduino GND ────────♦──────── Battery Pack GND
                       (one connection point)

NOTES:
1. Common ground: Arduino GND and battery pack GND connect at ONE point.
2. 1000µF capacitor must be wired close to the servo power distribution point.
3. Audio files go in "mp3" folder, FAT32 SD card, filename prefix "0001_".
4. Always wait 60 seconds after power-on before PIR sensor will behave reliably.
