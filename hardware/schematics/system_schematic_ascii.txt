NOTES:
1. Signal wire run lengths exceeding 0.5 meters must use shielded twisted pair.
2. Ensure Star Ground is positioned physically near the Arduino GND pin.
3. Decoupling capacitor (1000uF) must be physically soldered near the MG996R main hub block.
