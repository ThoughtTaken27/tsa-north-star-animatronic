2. POWER OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  9V BATTERY + BARREL JACK
        |
        v
  +-------------------+
  |   ARDUINO UNO     |
  |  onboard 5V rail  |      +---- 5V logic: PIR, DFPlayer, LEDs
  +-------------------+
                         |
  4xAA BATTERY PACK (6V) |
        |                |
        v                v
  +-------------------+  COMMON GROUND RAIL
  |  SERVO POWER RAIL |
  +-------------------+
        |
        +---- MG996R head servo (D9)
        +---- MG996R syringe-drive servo (D6)
        +---- SG90 lantern arm servo (D11)

  IMPORTANT:
  - Arduino GND and servo battery GND share one common ground rail.
  - Servo V+ is NOT connected to the Arduino 5V pin.
  - 1000uF 25V electrolytic capacitor across servo rail (polarized):

        servo rail +  ----||----  servo rail GND
                         1000uF
                         (long leg = +, stripe = -)

4. DFPLAYER MINI WIRING
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  DFPlayer VCC  ---> Arduino 5V
  DFPlayer GND  ---> Common ground
  DFPlayer TX   ---> Arduino D2 (direct)
  DFPlayer RX   ---> Voltage divider from Arduino D3:

      Arduino D3 ---- 680 ohm ----+---- DFPlayer RX
                                  |
                                 1k ohm
                                  |
                                 GND

      Output: 5V x (1000 / (680 + 1000)) = ~2.98V (safe for 3.3V RX)

  DFPlayer SPK1 ---> Speaker wire 1
  DFPlayer SPK2 ---> Speaker wire 2

  microSD card:
  - FAT32 format
  - folder: /mp3
  - file: /mp3/0001.mp3
  - 44.1kHz MP3 recommended


5. SENSORS AND LEDs
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  HC-SR501 PIR SENSOR (white Fresnel dome on green PCB):
  - VCC ---> Arduino 5V
  - OUT ---> Arduino D7
  - GND ---> Common ground
  - Two orange potentiometers: sensitivity + delay adjustment
  - 60-second warm-up period enforced in firmware after power-on

  North Star LED (orange, on backdrop):
  - Arduino D4 ---> 220 ohm ---> LED anode
  - LED cathode ---> Common ground

  Lantern LED (warm white, in wood-stick lantern on left arm):
  - Arduino D5 ---> 220 ohm ---> LED anode
  - LED cathode ---> Common ground


6. SERVOS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  MG996R HEAD TURN SERVO (mounted at top of PVC spine):
  - Signal ---> Arduino D9
  - V+     ---> 4xAA servo rail +
  - GND    ---> Common ground
  - Drives foam head through 1/4" vertical dowel
  - Modular FSM software limits: 55-125 degrees (home = 90)
  - Competition firmware: headAway = 90, headForward = 60

  MG996R SYRINGE-DRIVE SERVO (mounted on top of plywood base):
  - Signal ---> Arduino D6
  - V+     ---> 4xAA servo rail +
  - GND    ---> Common ground
  - Drives 60mL syringe through wooden popstick linkage
  - Syringe is horizontal, on top of base, zip-tied to right PVC + wood dowel
  - Modular FSM software limits: 40-130 degrees (home = 45)
  - Competition firmware: hydroRest = 70, hydroPush = 18

  SG90 LANTERN ARM SERVO (mounted on left PVC shoulder):
  - Signal ---> Arduino D11
  - V+     ---> 4xAA servo rail +
  - GND    ---> Common ground
  - Blue-bodied micro servo with white horn
  - Drives wood-stick lantern frame with warm white LED
  - Modular FSM software limits: 60-125 degrees (home = 70)
  - Competition firmware: leftDown = 0, leftUp = 90


7. PHYSICAL FRAME SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

  - Base:       12" x 12" x 3/4" plywood with wooden dowel feet
  - Spine:      1/2" PVC pipe, ~14" tall, in floor flange screwed to base
  - Shoulders:  PVC T-junction at spine top, 4" PVC pipes to elbows
  - Head:       6" foam ball, painted burnt sienna, on 1/4" dowel
  - Neck post:  1/4" round wooden dowel, ~4" tall, on MG996R servo horn
  - Right arm:  5/16" wood dowel driven by 60mL syringe (MG996R on base); pivots on a plain metal pin through a drilled hole at the shoulder (no powered servo at the hinge)
  - Left arm:   1/4" wood dowel with popsicle-stick lantern frame (SG90)
  - Wiring:     Multi-color Dupont jumpers, zip-tied to PVC spine
  - Shell:      Fabric/clothing removable in <10 seconds for judging
  - Fasteners:  Black nylon zip ties throughout

================================================================================
NOTES
================================================================================
1. Tie down servo wires so they cannot pull out during the removable-shell reveal.
2. Confirm capacitor polarity before powering the servo rail.
3. Power on early enough for the PIR warm-up window (60s) to finish.
4. Test the full sequence after transport, not just before packing.
5. Keep syringe mechanism visible to judges — the reveal is part of scoring.
6. The right arm dowel pivots on a plain metal pin at the shoulder; the syringe
   pushes one end so the arm rotates around the pin to raise the pointing end.
   No servo or motor is used at the hinge.
